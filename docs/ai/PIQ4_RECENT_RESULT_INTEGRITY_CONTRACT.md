# PIQ-4.2 recent Personal Lab result integrity

The previously attested active/soon-scheduled cohort is separate from completed
experiments: finalization ordinarily changes `active` to `completed` or
`inconclusive`. For present-day personalized weight/energy questions only,
screen finalized results whose effective end dates fall within **28 Health-local
calendar days**, inclusive of today.

The read is limited to 100 results, validates dates (no future or out-of-window
records), result classification, attestation, stopped-for-safety flag, protocol
version matching, expected parent experiment status and duplicate valid results.
The maintenance decision fingerprint now includes recent result identities,
status/invalidation, classifications, protocol requirements, requirement
evaluations and evidence fingerprints, under its existing twice-checked revision.

A direct recorded observation requires a valid `completed_interpretable` result,
explicitly followed protocol, no safety stop, and a typed direct weight/calorie
outcome requirement with evaluation status `available`. Low adherence,
invalidated results and inconclusive classifications do not count. Other
interpretable results are not presumed irrelevant to weight. Zero means no
finalized results in *this window*, not absence of other experiments or history.

Only aggregate counts and review state enter non-substantive AI evidence. No
owner IDs, dates, raw values, hypothesis, title or protocol text are exposed.
Even an interpretable recorded observation **does not establish direction,
effect magnitude, efficacy, causality, target adequacy, preserved strength or
permission for a calorie adjustment**. No database migration, live model usage
or production deployment.
